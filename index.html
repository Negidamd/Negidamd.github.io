Research Program

Precision medicine for neurodegenerative diseases through biomarker integration and computational methods

My research aims to improve care for patients with Parkinson's disease, Lewy body dementia, and Alzheimer's disease through precision medicine approaches. Disease-modifying trials often struggle because heterogeneous populations are treated as uniform entities. I work to address this challenge by integrating multimodal biomarkers—neuroimaging, EEG, fluid biomarkers—to help stratify patients into biologically meaningful subtypes.

Theme 1

Cognitive Fluctuations & Cholinergic Systems in Lewy Body Disease

Developing EEG-based biomarkers for cognitive fluctuations using NASA's Psychomotor Vigilance Test combined with resting-state and task-based EEG. Developed an MRI-based approach to subtype PD-MCI based on cholinergic nucleus 4 degeneration markers.

EEG-fMRI Integration Neuromodulation Cholinergic Targeting
Theme 2

Pathology-Driven Stratification (ATNM Framework)

Extended the Alzheimer's ATN framework to include Memory impairment (ATNM) and synuclein-vascular components (ATN-SV). Integrates α-synuclein quantification, plasma biomarkers, and MRI vascular measures for pathology classification.

Biomarker Integration Trial Enrichment Precision Therapeutics
Theme 3

Data-Driven PD Subtyping & Progression Modeling

Conducted comprehensive analysis of PD progression across clinical, pathological, and data-driven subtypes using 10-year PPMI data. Developed disease-duration-specific percentiles for prospective diffuse-malignant identification at diagnosis.

Machine Learning Longitudinal Modeling Clinical Translation
