Research Program

Cholinergic imaging, with disease subtyping and progression modeling, to stratify Parkinson's disease and Alzheimer's disease and related dementias for trials

Parkinson's disease (PD), dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB), and Alzheimer's disease (AD) are heterogeneous in their clinical features, underlying pathology, and progression. Enrolling biologically diverse patients into trials of target-specific therapies can dilute treatment effects. My program addresses this heterogeneity along two connected lines. The main line is cholinergic imaging: it measures degeneration of the nucleus basalis of Meynert (Ch4), which supplies most cholinergic input to the cortex, is shared across all three diseases, and is the biological rationale for cholinesterase inhibitors—yet response to these drugs varies widely. A complementary line, disease subtyping and progression modeling, identifies the patients who progress fastest and makes that classification usable in real time for trial screening. Together, they aim to match patients to the therapies most likely to help them, from cholinesterase inhibitors to muscarinic agonists, Ch4 deep brain stimulation, and gene therapy.

Research Plan: The Negida Lab

The lab will focus on scalable Ch4 imaging markers for trial enrichment. Three goals test whether these markers can identify the patients most likely to benefit from cholinergic therapies.

  1. 1
    Goal 1

    Generalize the Ch4 signature across diseases

    Extend the gray-matter and free-water signature to AD, DLB, and PDD in 3,611 participants from five cohorts (VCU, ADNI, NACC, PPMI, PDBP); test links to attentional and executive deficits and progression from MCI to dementia, benchmarked against hippocampal volume, global atrophy, and a non-cholinergic control region.

    Years 1–2
  2. 2
    Goal 2

    Establish what the MRI signature measures

    With collaborators in cholinergic PET imaging, benchmark the Ch4 signature against [18F]FEOBV PET of the vesicular acetylcholine transporter, and test whether preserved Ch4–cortical connectivity despite structural loss marks compensation.

    Years 3–5
  3. 3
    Goal 3

    Link Ch4 markers to treatment response

    Target-trial emulation of cholinesterase inhibitor outcomes by baseline Ch4 integrity, informing a randomized cholinergic therapy trial stratified by Ch4 integrity with a prespecified treatment-by-biomarker interaction.

    Years 1–2; randomized trial in years 5–7

Long-term vision. An automated MRI pipeline that stages Ch4–cortical cholinergic degeneration in individual patients with PD or ADRD and identifies those best suited to cholinergic trials—a framework that could also inform Ch4 deep brain stimulation, gene therapy, and neurotrophic trials, where the remaining target tissue may determine benefit. Approved access to ten multisite imaging cohorts supports the first two years without new recruitment or scanner time.

Featured Work

Published

Low Ch4 gray-matter density marks a more severe, faster-declining PD-MCI group

Using voxel-based morphometry and probabilistic Ch4 maps in 162 PPMI participants with PD-MCI, we identified 40 with low Ch4 density (below −1 SD). They had worse MDS-UPDRS, autonomic, and olfactory scores and reached cognitive milestones sooner (log-rank p = 0.002). The classifier is released as an open research tool.

Parkinsonism & Related Disorders, 2025 →

Illustration: Julich-Brain Ch4 on the MNI152 template (not a figure from the article).

Published

Slower resting EEG rhythms flag cognitive fluctuations in Lewy body disease

Eyes-closed resting EEG in 53 patients across the Lewy body spectrum. Patients with fluctuations had lower dominant frequency, dominant-alpha prevalence, and individual alpha peak frequency after adjusting for age, sex, and MoCA; an elastic-net model detected fluctuations with AUC 0.86. The topographic maps show mean dominant frequency at every electrode: about 6–7 Hz in patients with fluctuations versus about 8 Hz in those without.

Alzheimer's Research & Therapy, 2026 → CC BY 4.0

Published

Diffuse-malignant PD progresses fastest and could halve trial sample sizes

Over 10 years of PPMI follow-up, we compared clinical, pathological, and data-driven subtypes on milestones across six functional domains using Cox regression. Diffuse-malignant patients reached 63.0% of milestones (PIGD 55.6%, body-first 54.0%), and in trial simulations enrolling them cut the required sample size by about 50%.

npj Parkinson's Disease, 2026 → CC BY 4.0 · cropped

Published

Year-specific percentiles enable real-time PD subtyping for trial enrollment

Percentile thresholds for motor, cognitive, RBD, and autonomic scores, set for each of the first 5 years after diagnosis (PPMI, N = 1,030), classify patients prospectively each year. Diffuse-malignant PD reached a first progression milestone fastest (HR 3.03, 95% CI 2.30–3.97, vs mild motor-predominant).

Mov Disord Clin Pract, 2026 → CC BY-NC-ND 4.0

Published

LRRK2 and GBA1 map onto different PD subtypes

Variant frequencies were compared across four subtyping frameworks in 1,390 genotyped PPMI patients with FDR correction. LRRK2 variants were enriched in α‑synuclein SAA-negative PD (37.1% vs 10.2%) and GBA1 variants in diffuse-malignant PD (14.0% vs 5.9% in mild motor-predominant); the tremor/gait split showed no signal that survived correction.

Genes (Basel), 2026 → CC BY 4.0

Pending Publication

Ch4 atrophy and free water independently predict cognition in PD

We combined T1-weighted and free-water diffusion MRI in 92 participants (controls, PD-NC, PD-MCI, PDD). Ch4 gray-matter density was lowest and free water highest in PDD. Only these two Ch4 measures independently predicted cognition, and together they separated PDD from PD without dementia (exploratory AUC 0.93).

medRxiv preprint, 2026 →

TBI mortality across 29 countries does not follow a development gradient

This prospective cohort enrolled 2,165 patients with operative or nonoperative TBI at 100 hospitals. In inverse-probability-weighted mixed-effects models, mortality showed no dose-response with the Human Development Index; injury mechanism, demographics, and prehospital access drove the disparities. Role: co-founder of the Global NeuroSurg Research Collaborative; co-principal investigator, steering committee, and writing group.

Ongoing Work

Ch4-salience connectivity relates to cognition beyond atrophy and free water

Resting-state fMRI in 97 participants (16 controls, 81 with PD) showed Ch4 connectivity with salience, action-mode, and language networks declining across PD cognitive stages (FDR q < 0.05). Salience connectivity added 8.6 percentage points of explained variance in cognition beyond Ch4 gray-matter density and free water, although the effect weakened in sensitivity analyses.

Negida-DTI-ALPS

Diffusion MRI · Open Source

DTI-ALPS pipeline for quantifying perivascular (glymphatic) diffusivity, using ANTs SyN nonlinear registration in place of the FSL-FLIRT tensor registration of the standard alps.sh toolbox, with T1-mediated boundary-based registration that corrects ALPS ROI misplacement in high head-rotation cases. Validated against the reference implementation across 106 diffusion-MRI datasets (r = 0.96 for mean ALPS). MIT-licensed.

Negida Research Academy

Founded in 2014, Negida Research Academy is a global virtual training platform in clinical research methods: study design, biostatistics, systematic reviews, meta-analysis, scientific writing, and grant preparation. It runs two structured cohort-based training camps each year, each training approximately 1,200 researchers over 8–12 weeks. Author of the Negida Handbook of Clinical Research.
