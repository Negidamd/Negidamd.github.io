Research Program

Precision Medicine for Neurodegeneration

Addressing disease heterogeneity through biomarker integration and computational innovation

My long-term goal is to transform the care of patients with Parkinson's disease, Lewy body dementia, and Alzheimer's disease through precision medicine approaches that enable early diagnosis, personalized treatment selection, and disease-modifying intervention. Disease-modifying trials consistently fail because we treat heterogeneous populations as uniform entities. My research addresses this gap by integrating multimodal biomarkers to stratify patients into biologically meaningful subtypes.

01

Cognitive Fluctuations & Cholinergic Systems in LBD

Developing EEG-based biomarkers for cognitive fluctuations using NASA's Psychomotor Vigilance Test combined with resting-state and task-based EEG. Pioneered MRI-based subtyping of PD-MCI with cholinergic nucleus 4 degeneration markers.

EEG-fMRI Integration Neuromodulation Cholinergic Targeting
02

Pathology-Driven Stratification (ATNM Framework)

Extended the Alzheimer's ATN framework to include Memory impairment (ATNM) and synuclein-vascular components (ATN-SV). Integrates α-synuclein quantification, plasma biomarkers, and MRI vascular measures for dominant pathology classification.

Biomarker Integration Trial Enrichment Precision Therapeutics
03

Data-Driven PD Subtyping & Progression Modeling

Led comprehensive analysis of PD progression across clinical, pathological, and data-driven subtypes using 10-year PPMI data. Developed disease-duration-specific percentiles enabling prospective identification of diffuse-malignant patients at diagnosis.

Machine Learning Longitudinal Modeling Clinical Translation
