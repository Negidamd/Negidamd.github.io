Cholinergic Nucleus 4 (Ch4) Normative Calculator

Convert an individual’s Ch4 gray-matter volume into a normative W-score — how far it deviates from the volume expected in cognitively healthy adults of the same age, sex, and head size — and see where the individual falls within the healthy reference distribution.

Research demonstrator · runs entirely in your browser
Research and educational use only. This is a demonstration of a published normative model. It is not a medical device, is not validated for clinical use, and must not be used for diagnosis or patient management. A valid W-score requires Ch4 gray-matter volume measured with the same pipeline as the study (CAT12 under SPM; JulichBrain maximum-probability map at 50%; partial-volume gray-matter volume in mL; TIV from CAT12). The model was calibrated by cross-validation and has not yet been externally validated. All values are computed locally — no data is transmitted or stored.

Position in the healthy reference distribution

The bell curve is the calibrated normal reference (W ~ standard normal in controls). The shaded tail is the atrophy zone (W ≤ −1.645, the 5th centile). The patient’s value is marked.

Model. Two-stage location–scale normative model of Ch4 gray-matter volume fitted in 6,311 cognitively healthy adults. Location: predicted = 0.1378 + 0.000604·age − 1.219×10⁻⁵·age² + 0.00271·[male] + 9.544×10⁻⁵·TIV. Scale: σ(age) = 1.887 · exp(−4.8134 + 0.006070·age). W = (observed − predicted) / σ(age). For a scanner site not in the training sample, the site random intercept is set to zero (as here). Coefficients are Supplementary Table S1 of the study.

Citation. Negida A. A Normative Modeling Study of Cholinergic Nucleus 4 Atrophy Across the Lewy Body and Alzheimer Disease Spectra. (in preparation).   ORCID 0000-0001-5363-6369 · negidamd.github.io
